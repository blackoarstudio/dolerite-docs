# Support

## Contact us

**[Open a support request](CONTACT_FORM_URL)**

The form is the fastest route and the only one we monitor. It is public — you do not
need an account to use it.

## What to include

The more of this you send with the first message, the fewer rounds it takes:

- Your **store URL** (`your-store.myshopify.com`).
- The **page** where you see the problem, as a link.
- What you **expected** and what you **saw** instead.
- A **screenshot or a short screen recording**. A recording is worth several messages
  for anything that moves.
- Which **browser and device** you are on, if it only happens on one of them.
- A **preview link**, if the theme is not published yet. In the admin: **Online Store →
  Themes → ⋯ → Share preview**, then paste the link.

If the problem is on an unpublished theme, say so — we cannot see it otherwise.

## Response times

We reply to every request within **two business days**, usually sooner. Business days
are Monday to Friday.

If you have not heard back after two business days, send the request again — a small
number of messages are lost to spam filters every year, in both directions.

## What support covers

**Covered:**

- Anything in the theme not behaving as this documentation describes.
- Help finding the setting that does what you are trying to do.
- Bug reports, including ones we end up fixing in a later release.
- Questions about installing, updating and setting up the theme.

**Not covered:**

- Custom code, and debugging changes made to the theme's code. We will look, and tell
  you what we find, but we cannot maintain modifications.
- Third-party apps. If an app conflicts with the theme, tell us which one and we will
  investigate our side of it.
- Design, copywriting and store setup services.
- Shopify's own platform, billing and account issues — those go to
  [Shopify Support](https://help.shopify.com).

## Reporting a bug

Bug reports are welcome and taken seriously. What helps most:

1. The steps that reproduce it, in order.
2. Whether it happens on a **fresh install** of the theme with default settings. If you
   can reproduce it on an unmodified copy, say so — that tells us immediately that it is
   ours.
3. Whether it started after a Shopify update, an app install, or a theme update.

## Theme updates

Updates are published as a new zip. Existing customers get access to every update for
the theme they bought at no extra cost.

Shopify does not merge settings between uploads, so an update means: upload the new
version as an unpublished theme, set it up alongside the live one, and publish when it
matches. [Getting started](getting-started.md#updating-to-a-newer-version-of-dolerite)
walks through it.
